Native Azure Databricks app with a Multi-Agent Supervisor, Genie and Knowledge Assistant sub-agents, On-Behalf-Of (OBO) authorization, and automatic charts in Agent Mode.

What it solves

One chat for operational data and corporate documents, with streaming, history, light/dark theme, and web/mobile use.

Security

Databricks Apps auth, group permissions, end-user token (OBO), and proactive blocking of unauthorized delegations.

Genie agents (SQL)

Knowledge Assistants (RAG)

Domain document assistants (policies, PMO, talent, etc.) with citations and Unity Catalog / AI Gateway governance.
